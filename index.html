Frequently asked questions

What does t2i do?

t2i builds AI models. AANMA supports doctors with clinical decisions. Calvee adapts each chapter to the student, from class 1 to higher education.

Do the models replace doctors or teachers?

No. AANMA suggests and the doctor decides and signs. Calvee builds the chapter and the teacher leads the class.

How do we use a model?

Healthcare platforms connect to AANMA by API. Schools, colleges, universities and parents can bring Calvee to their students. Write to create@the2amidea.com.

Where are the models built?

In Bengaluru, India.
