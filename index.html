What changes

Five shifts we are building towards.

  1. 01

    From one test to the whole person.

    Decisions will start from everything that is known about a patient, a student or a citizen: not one scan, one exam score or one form.

  2. 02

    From the same for everyone to the right thing for each.

    The treatment plan, the lesson and the public service will fit the person in front of them.

  3. 03

    From waiting to reaching first.

    Help will go first to the patient who is most at risk, the student who needs it most and the citizen whose case cannot wait.

  4. 04

    From a few centres to everywhere.

    The expertise of the best hospitals, schools and public offices will reach every city and every remote town.

  5. 05

    From replacing people to backing them.

    The doctor will still decide. The teacher will still lead. The official will still sign. AI will give each of them more time for people.

Our promise

People stay in charge.

AI does the first read and the hard sorting. People make every decision that matters.

A person decides.

AI reads, sorts and drafts. A doctor, a teacher or an official makes the call, and their name goes on it.

Consent first.

Health, learning and civic records open only with the consent of the person they belong to, or as the law allows.

Reasons, not black boxes.

Every suggestion shows what it is based on, so a professional can check it, change it or reject it.

Built to fit each country.

Each deployment follows the country’s own languages, laws, curricula and health standards, and keeps its data where the law says it must stay.

For investors

Three markets. One platform.

Healthcare, education and government are among the largest areas of spending in every economy, and among the least served by AI. 2i builds one platform of models, safeguards and workflows that serves all three.

01

One core, three markets.

The same engine reads records, sorts by urgency and drafts the next step for a hospital, a school or a public office.

02

Trust by design.

A person signs every decision, every suggestion shows its reasons and every record opens only with consent. Each step can be audited.

03

Built to deploy country by country.

Languages, laws, curricula and data rules are settings, not rebuilds. Each new country runs on the same platform.
