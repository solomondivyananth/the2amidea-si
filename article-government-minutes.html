A government that answers in minutes

Permits, benefits and certificates can move at the speed of a message, if every step stays on record.

2i team · 19 November 2026

For most citizens, government is not a policy. It is a form, a queue and a wait. A permit, a benefit or a certificate can take weeks, and most of that time the file sits in a pile, waiting for someone to read it and send it on.

About 850 million people in the world have no official proof of identity, according to the World Bank. For them, every public service starts with a wall. For everyone else, it starts with a delay.

Read, check, route

The first part of most public work is the same: read the request, check it against the rules and send it to the right desk. That part is slow for people and fast for AI. A model can read a request as it arrives, check that the documents are complete, flag what is missing and send the case to the official who decides.

The cases that cannot wait, such as a medical benefit or an emergency permit, go to the top, as the most urgent scan does in a hospital.

The official still signs

Public decisions must be fair and open to challenge. So the official still decides and signs. Every step is on record: what the model read, what it suggested and who made the decision. Records open only with the citizen’s consent or as the law allows, and the data stays where the law says it must stay.
