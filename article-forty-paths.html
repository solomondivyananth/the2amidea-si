One class, forty students, forty paths

A syllabus is the same for every student. The way to it does not have to be.

2i team · 19 November 2026

Every teacher knows the problem. In a class of forty, some students finished the chapter last week and some have not understood the one before it. The lesson goes at one speed, and that speed is wrong for most of the room.

The world needs 44 million more teachers by 2030, according to UNESCO. More teachers will help. But even a full staff room cannot give each student a lesson of their own. That is where AI can carry part of the load.

The same syllabus, a different way in

CALVEE builds each chapter for the student who reads it. The facts and the learning goals stay the same. The pace, the order and the examples change. A student who learns best from a story gets a story. A student who is stuck gets one more step before the next idea. A student who is ahead gets a harder task.

Examples matter more than they seem. A student learns faster from an example from their own world: their city, their sport, their kitchen. CALVEE can choose the example for each student, inside the curriculum the school already follows.

The teacher leads

The teacher sees the whole class on one screen: who is ahead, who is stuck and who needs a word today. The board shows the shared lesson. Each student works on their own path. The teacher stays in charge of the class, and gets back the time that marking and repeating took.

You can open the CALVEE live classroom and see a lesson for yourself.
