How it works

From first call to every site.

  1. 1

    Talk

    We learn how your organisation works today and where the waiting is.

  2. 2

    Scope

    Together we choose one service, one queue or one class to start with.

  3. 3

    Pilot

    2i runs beside your people. They decide. We measure what changes.

  4. 4

    Grow

    When the results hold, we take it to more sites, more subjects or more services.
