Overview

A scan shows only part of the story. The same shadow on a chest X-ray means one thing for a 30-year-old with a cough, and another for a 70-year-old on blood thinners.

Capabilities

Reads the scan with the history

AANMA reads X-ray, CT and MRI images together with the linked record, lab results and medicines.

Sorts cases by urgency

Each case gets an urgency level, so the most serious patient reaches a doctor first.

Suggests the next step

AANMA suggests what to do next for the patient, such as more tests or a referral to a specialist.

Flags allergies and risks

AANMA checks the record for allergies, current medicines and past conditions, and shows an alert before the doctor decides.

Drafts the report

For each scan, AANMA writes a first draft of the report. The doctor edits it and signs it.

Learns from doctors

Doctors review and correct its outputs, and each correction goes back into training.

Use cases

Emergency rooms

A patient’s history sits in hospital files, lab systems, pharmacy records and health apps that do not share data. AANMA brings the relevant parts into one view, so the doctor sees allergies, medicines and past conditions before deciding.

Radiology queues

AANMA reads each scan first and sorts the queue by urgency, so the most serious cases reach a radiologist first. It also drafts the report for the radiologist to edit and sign.

Healthcare platforms

Hospital software, health apps, insurers, PACS vendors and teleradiology networks add AANMA to the products they already sell, without building a model of their own.

Medical colleges

Residents compare their reads and next steps with AANMA’s, and faculty use it for teaching cases and research.

Healthcare brands

Device and pharma brands offer AANMA inside their own products, under their own name.

Responsible use

AANMA supports the doctor’s decision and never makes it. Every output goes to a doctor, and every report carries a doctor’s signature.

Patient data stays in India and is handled under the DPDP Act. Records open only with the patient’s consent, and every access goes into an audit log.

Frequently asked questions

Does AANMA replace the doctor?

No. AANMA suggests an urgency level, a next step and alerts. A doctor reviews, edits and signs every output.

Why read the history with the scan?

The right next step depends on the patient. Age, past conditions, lab results and medicines change what a finding on a scan means.

Which scans does AANMA read?

X-ray, CT and MRI.

How does a platform connect to AANMA?

By API. The platform sends the scan and the linked record, and shows AANMA’s output inside its own product.

Where is patient data kept?

In India, under the DPDP Act. Access needs the patient’s consent and is logged.
